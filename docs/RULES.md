# Rules

## Your goal

Keep your foundation's service (a soup kitchen, madrasa, mosque or fountain) open until the level's target year. In free play the target is 600 years.

## Setup

- Split **100 akçe** among the bathhouse, inn, farmland and cash. What each one does is in the **Buildings** tab.
- Set part of the income aside in the **reserve fund**. This money pays for repairs on bad days.
- Choose a **trustee**: the honest one is safe; the skilful one earns a little more but sometimes steals from the fund.

## One round (10 years)

1. The properties earn money, and it goes into the foundation chest.
2. A share goes to the reserve fund.
3. The buildings' upkeep is paid.
4. The rest pays for the service. Anything left over collects as **building money**.

**▶ PLAY** lets the years pass by themselves and stops at a hard moment. **+10 years** plays one round.

## Bad days

In an earthquake, fire or epidemic the game stops and asks a short two-choice question:

- **Right answer:** the disaster is put out and the damage is halved.
- **Wrong answer:** you lose a life (you start with 3 ❤️ and get one back every century).

Then you decide:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Repair with fund** | Everything is fixed; the reserve fund goes down. |
| **Repair with cash** | Cash repairs what it can cover; the rest is lost. |
| **Cut back the service** | You spend nothing; the service runs at half for a while and the buildings recover slowly. |

Drought (fields give no crops), war (the inn earns nothing) and inflation (cash and fund melt) happen by themselves. A bountiful year, caravan season and a benefactor's gift are good news.

## Grow the city

- Press **See the city**, tap a plot with a yellow **+** and build with building money.
- Areas open over time: Bazaar in year 50, Neighbourhood 150, Villages 250, Caravan road 350.
- Visit a real monument and collect a **stamp** in your passport, and areas open sooner and your foundation gets a gift.
- Tap the people on the map to read short facts about real monuments; some leave akçe in your building money.

## Losing and winning

- If the service cannot be paid for **3 rounds in a row**, the foundation collapses. On Easy it takes 4.
- Reach the target year to pass the level; the monument's card goes into your album and the next level opens.
- If the chest is never empty, you earn more stars.

| Level | City · service | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · The first soup kitchen | Bursa · soup kitchen | 200 years |
| 2 · Dry years | Konya · fountain | 300 years, 1 new building |
| 3 · A madrasa on the road | Hatay · madrasa | 400 years, 2 new buildings |
| 4 · City of earthquakes | Hatay · mosque | 500 years, 3 new buildings |

## Four golden rules

1. Don't put all your money in one place.
2. Always save a share in the reserve fund.
3. Choose the trustee for honesty.
4. Keep the service only as big as your income can carry.

## Words to know

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Vakıf | Property set aside so that a good work lasts forever, and the body that runs it |
| Akar | Property that earns income for the foundation: bathhouse, inn, farmland, arasta… |
| Hayrat | A building that serves the public for free: soup kitchen, madrasa, mosque, fountain |
| Mütevelli | The trustee who runs the foundation |
| Akçe | The Ottoman silver coin; the game's currency |
